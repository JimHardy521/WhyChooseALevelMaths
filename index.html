About this website

This website helps students explore where A Level Maths can lead. Each postcard features someone who studied maths and went on to use their skills in a wide range of careers and industries.

Click on a photo to discover their story: why they chose A Level Maths, what they enjoyed, and how maths helps them in their job today.

As a father of two young girls, and as an A Level Maths Lead at a large, outstanding school in Nottinghamshire, I am particularly interested in highlighting how studying A Level Maths has supported women in their careers.
