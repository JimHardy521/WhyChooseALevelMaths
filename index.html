Thanks For Visiting...

I am the A Level Maths Lead at Rushcliffe Spencer Academy - a large, outstanding secondary school in Nottinghamshire, and am interested in how A Level Mathematics has proved interesting and useful to others.

Whilst there is an insignificant gender gap at GCSE, the number of boys who then study A Level Mathematics far exceeds that of girls. In my role, and as a father of two young girls, I am particularly interested in showcasing women who have progressed with A Level Maths.

Thank you so much for visiting the site, and I hope you consider contributing.
